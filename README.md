# Photo Nook 📸

**The temporary camera app that keeps your main photo library and iCloud storage clutter-free.**

Photo Nook gives you a dedicated, isolated space to capture photos and videos you only need temporarily—like parking spots, temporary receipts, event agendas, or quick reminders—without bloating your primary iPhone camera roll or paying for extra iCloud storage.

---

## Key Features

- **In-App Camera Capture:** Snap photos and record videos directly within Photo Nook. All media is kept in a private local sandbox separate from your iOS Photos app.
- **Time-To-Live (TTL) Auto-Cleanup:** Media automatically deletes after a set period (defaulted to **7 days**). Enjoy automatic storage management without manual deletion.
- **Save iCloud Storage:** Prevent temporary snaps from taking up valuable cloud backup space.
- **100% Private & Local:** Your photos and videos never leave your device. No external servers, no cloud tracking, and no data harvesting.

## Support & FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### **Q: Are my photos uploaded to any servers?**
**A:** No. All media captured in Photo Nook stays entirely on your device within the app's local sandbox storage.

#### **Q: What happens when the TTL timer expires?**
**A:** Once the set time-to-live period (e.g., 7 days) elapses, the app automatically deletes the media file from your device to reclaim storage space.

#### **Q: Can I save a photo permanently if I decide I want to keep it?**
**A:** Yes! You can manually export or save any photo or video to your main iOS Photo Library before its TTL timer expires.

#### **Q: Does Photo Nook require an account or sign-in?**
**A:** No account is needed. Just download the app and start taking temporary photos right away.
